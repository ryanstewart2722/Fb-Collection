Privacy

Last updated November 2026. The Museum of Mini Skateboards (museumofminiskateboards.org) is a small hobby project run by Ryan Stewart.

What we collect

If you make an account: your email address, the username you pick, and an optional display name and bio. If you sign in with Google, we receive only your email address and name from Google. We never see your Google password.

If you add boards to your collection or submit one to the museum: the details you type and the photos you upload.

What other people can see

Your username, display name, bio, and the boards and photos in your collection are public at museumofminiskateboards.org/u/yourname once approved. You can make your collection private from your page. Your email address is never shown to other people.

How it is used and stored

We use your email only to sign you in and to reply if you write to us. We do not sell your information and we do not run ads or trackers. Account data and photos are stored with Supabase. Sign-in emails are sent through Resend. Collections are reviewed by the site owner before photos appear publicly.

Deleting your data

Email contact@museumofminiskateboards.org and we will delete your account, collection and photos.
